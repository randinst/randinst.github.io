## Anti-parasitism <span style="font-size: 0.9em; color: #8FA3B0;">*noun*</span>

: a relationship in which the survival of one organism depends on the perishing of another, the dependant being the anti-parasite. 

## Anti-determinism <span style="font-size: 0.9em; color: #8FA3B0;">*noun*</span>

: in computer science, the view favouring statistical and unpredictable outcomes instead of known and defined ones.

## Idiot strength <span style="font-size: 0.9em; color: #8FA3B0;">*noun*</span>

: the property, ability, or force of a powerful state to enact theft upon a not-so-powerful state. This theft may be of land, bodies, souls, pride, dignity, or anything that is available. 

## Old man strength <span style="font-size: 0.9em; color: #8FA3B0;">*noun*</span>

: the property, ability, or force of a man to bend the masses entirely to his demands when he has at the very most ten (10) years left before his demise.

## Nosejob <span style="font-size: 0.9em; color: #8FA3B0;">*noun*</span>

: a piece of work produced using your nose.

## Fake noose <span style="font-size: 0.9em; color: #8FA3B0;">*noun*</span>

: a loop formed in a rope that is found in one's imagination.

## Communitification <span style="font-size: 0.9em; color: #8FA3B0;">*noun*</span>

: the process of turning a subject's followers into a community without regard to its necessity or consequences.

## Absolutism <span style="font-size: 0.9em; color: #8FA3B0;">*verb*</span>

: a religious thought born in years AD based on its complete refusal to acknowledge the existence or non-existence of an object. Examples in science include but are not limited to: Navier-Stokes, Schrödinger’s cat.

## Anti-feudalism <span style="font-size: 0.9em; color: #8FA3B0;">*proverb*</span>

: a sociopolitical system characterized by its lack of hierarchy, in which there exist only landowners who have gotten around their need for subordinates in maintaining the land and producing from it. 

## Patient <span style="font-size: 0.9em; color: #8FA3B0;">*noun*</span>

: an organism displaying excessive amounts of patience toward a persistent attacker or parasite, to an extent where no resistance is attempted.
