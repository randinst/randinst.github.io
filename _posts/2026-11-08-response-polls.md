**Nothing is Political**

Neither trees, nor their twirling knees<br>
Neither traffic as a whole, nor the candy that you stole<br>
Oh, but you digress and say, *How about the armament soufflé?*<br>
Guns, their blazers—simply itch to try their own invented way!<br>
Nothing shared or malice-driven,<br>
Just a curiosity that causes storm.<br>
Not that it is anyone's selected form...<br>
Maybe bites, sometimes stings,<br>
Without asking, it proposes rings.<br>
*Dance with us*, they say;<br>
Okay, but the dancefloor isn't paid.<br>
Builder without purpose had those bricks all laid.<br>
Sure, the foundation is now drivel,<br>
and the masses aren't civil,<br>
But point me to an incentivizéd fool<br>
and I'll show you just a temporary tool.<br>
A tool unused in a larger sense,<br>
A sense that knows no fence.<br>
A type of neutral orb that moves without an ask<br>
It never needs a reason to at large inform a task<br>
So we drift in it, with muds betwixt the legs<br>
Never there's a benefit in joining sects,<br>
which we haven't, but the motion still affects<br>
Never there's a time that is atypical,<br>
So know that everything's political
