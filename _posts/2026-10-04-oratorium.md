Jesus farted, and the world began.<br>
You have always known he is the Son of Sam.<br>
A trickster of the will, at his father's will.<br>
He asks mother, *when'll you write the will?*

Utterance of darkest lord,<br>
It finds the heavenest disciple bored.<br>
At this rate, David, Sam, and Rob, they<br>
Shall fumble even the bereftest yield array.

Joseph doesn't know what he's been saying.<br>
Stephen, Mary have been dating.<br>
As for townclerks, it's a different situation;<br>
Those now drift into elation.

Spoken past and cross the wielding motion,<br>
Psalms derived from mathematical commotion.<br>
*Die*, a commentator shouted,<br>
As he fell from Crucifixing Hill of Farmstead.

Clouds depunctured by a rustic nail,<br>
Shame, upon the heavens,<br>
Dancing trail.

*I do not demand for my return to Holy Rail*<br>
Screamed again the devastated sail'.
