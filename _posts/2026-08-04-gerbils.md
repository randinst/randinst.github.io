To whom I show restraint,<br> 
Or tangle riches in the mist,<br>
Voices, distant from my cyst<br>
Rabid they may never faint

Citizen before the pact<br>
Knoweth he how he must act<br>
Not without the quiet cheer, while dim<br>
Not before the Others sneer at him with sin

Tangles, dangles wish upon a sound gall-opus<br>
Silence, when it glows they shan't rope us<br>
Ah the imagery I must escape, do you feel the same, my trusting cape?

Forever more unnoticed lives your flaw<br>
But what if Others visioned what I saw

Medic scraped and tied your beaten drum<br>
Child of hazel, found in scum

For I only am a shadow, hence I know<br>
Part of you, part of two<br>
Ravaged by a fatal blow

A profounded history of you<br>
Told through fellow people too<br>
As amassed by criticality,<br>
My pen and papers; your lethality

Lined up suspects yield us force of Spanish bulls<br>
Won't be long 'till dance their cracked out skulls<br>
Whether it be sons our friends will sell,<br> 
Be it daughters of the Veln

Spite us all, or two, you remnant Church!<br>
That’s until I find your treasures to besmirch<br>
Leader leads with simple hand,<br>
Undisturbed by your demand<br>
Feeding a momentum utterly of heaviest sand,<br>
Finds the path to reinvigorate our birch

Many a demented bottomfeeder stand before us limpless, then they limp<br>
Kneecaps of their yesteryear cast out wider than the nation’s blimp<br>
*You* are in the way, in a time designed for giving with no disarray<br>
Shall there be no more vast East and tumultuous West,<br>
So that we can demonstrate us to ourselves from our uniquest crest?

Highness, and you still believe we have more years to spare?<br>
Chance of reaching gates to India—no longer it lays bare<br>
Circumstantial—what is seen or is believed—stories of the wind<br>
Highness, you must be aware—I am prepared, for I’m thick-skinned<br>
—mainland turbulence expects it, you don’t care?

---

Time! I finally receive the game, with permission from the sage<br>
Here they go, the fluid tongues in which I speak,<br>
Every day on the first page.<br>
Farther than they ever, just as countless bodies’ liquids they will reek

There is gladness to my heart, fellows’ve put a face to my own name!<br>
Never uninvited, not neglected—but cardinal, how go our operations?<br>
Good thus far, I don’t believe, I tell how it is. Have you looked at my creations…<br>
Highness, why you have to take away my claim?
