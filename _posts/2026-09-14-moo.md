I'm the moo-cow, of the field<br>
*Moo*, say I, untethered by the troths of man<br>
Would you look at how I walk—<br>
All at once, if god is watching—<br>
Horns toward him, as if I'm stalking<br>
A haired centipede that I am,<br>
I never need no pants,<br>
and I'm not too fond of hungry ants<br>
Hiding, biting, in the grass – <br>
The overbearing saint<br>
My dirty tail is no match for the<br>
Insects' religious restraint<br>
To whose timetable I belong?<br>
And what kind of question is this song?<br>
Let us better look again at this, my<br> 
Undecipherable four-legged nipple<br>
An organism of its own<br>
Can I move it? I don't know<br>
There's a lot of grass to mow<br>
Instead of waiting for the human tow<br>
Of my glossy body that has won<br>
Town Contest twice in a row<br>
Or was it just a scale?<br>
Why then did it not turn my skin fright pale?
