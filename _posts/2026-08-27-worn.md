You are here, you are now, you're important<br>
Where you're from, detail of indent'fication – your ability<br>
Stranded but not bound, you strike for recognition of the sound mobility<br>
For whose sake? A simple answer: The king that died before the other king<br>
Onlookers, they too, die without'ving heard Sting<br>
In plantations, lands of federality, lie the skulls of those who've played<br>
Not eroding vital culture, adding, though, in fact<br>
In this current moment swindle Opium addicted kinds<br>
I, for one, like you though should, point to father and grandfather's worn soft lapel<br>
Squeezes like a sponge, not like those of other kinds that leave the splinter in the finger<br>
Here it sits on walls the ancestors have placed<br>
I watch them every day unfazed<br>
A simple pleasure: 'member those that've never glazed<br>
Over dicey thought and act endeavour t’show the other kinds that grazed<br>
Interest in quiet order leaves my belly past the border<br>
Ship that never sailed has me wanting to pre-board her<br>
Oils and spices brought from ponderous and primate wards<br>
I have never fought with swords<br>
Likeness is the preciousest affair I have, don't confuse my stature with those other kinds that've starved<br>
Either hills and churches stand, or I break upon my levied arm<br>
Dice, they might have played, in those of olden mightful days<br>
Those that lay before the quest'ning man<br>
Intermediaries of historic land<br>
Excitement's what I really want—not to seem a wart without my worth<br>
Quarters I have never occupied before; that must be because of honor, grace, and war<br>
Reap—I'd never—fruit of ordinals that precede my moament<br>
Singers, they have melodied 'ver the condonement<br>
But I stand like this, and why don't you?<br>
I shall send a tongue or two to smite the incest that you do
