*One*

Root, stem, flower, seed, and leave<br>
We will sure now know what to believe<br>
That the branching of our tree<br>
Continues path of dedicated spree<br>

There are circles round it all around<br>
Manifesting debtors from outside the town<br>
Vipers, even those would never roll as such<br>
Will there ever be a more deserving bunch?

The finality runs deep in blood and stone<br>
There is not a single detail more to hone<br>
Depth-indulgent creatures roaming sore<br>
and waiting<br>
For themselves to wash up on our shore

*Two*

Listener of half assembled chord,<br>
Hesist, don't drift, then have it all deplored!<br>
Teacher of the mind and soul<br>
Bothered not to raise you under sole

Infer, and others deter,<br>
As there be a maximal insistence to bow low<br>
Signify and amplify<br>
Or then else resulting viciousness will flow

Or then else...<br>
Or then else...<br>
O...

*Three*

Branching gardens, leaking quorum<br>
Distances and queries into sum<br>
Run they twisting and delisting<br>
Opionated turns avidly insisting

There's a ranch in this branch<br>
A cavern in this tavern<br>
Daisies and a pit of flames belong<br>
There is also a dissolving pot<br>
Running her forever hot

Richest tapestry, they'd say<br>
Forming armament soufflé<br>
Oils sans spices yearning for another day

Retinas, ballooning corn<br>
It's as if the form will not adjourn<br>
Busyness and riddance<br>
Banging on the door

*Four*

Nightingale adjourned the song<br>
Godly verb'rance vests the corn<br>
Turnip, its gold plating, burns<br>
Acid rivers carry urns

No resultant even perished<br>
Here the comments run asteam<br>
There exists not one prologue to cherish<br>
Dim for one and light for some

Fierce in the initials, shrill before a storm<br>
Captivating in its lit'ral form<br>
Eventuality though implovision<br>
Tickles not just one observant lord

Rum has flowed and flown<br>
Icing on a hardwood floor<br>
Boils it inward, some will own<br>
A filthiest version of all gore
