The third eye, my third eye,<br>
Divinitive, but not profound.<br>
Raises questions on each sound,<br>
Tampers with sensorial committees,<br>
It then also wanders past the platform.

There's no say in what it catches,<br>
Beneficial, only in divine proverbial form,<br>
Works the Second Eye to orchestrate, deform.<br>
Loses little composition in a rainstorm.

 The dimwitted and dimwitched ought to comprehend,<br>
There is no room for analytic thought to mend.

Primordial dances, those’ve been all figured out.<br>
Soups in flesh, in active and in drying flesh.<br>
There’s no say in what it catches,<br>
Third eye dances unobserved and wíthout figure.

Reasonables say, *how’d you know without’ving caught it?*<br>
They don’t know, it’s the Third Eye that’s speaking.<br>
It reaches t’ward the lines that hold the soup,<br>
Intersecting and intangling, a circlish loop.

Sprouting and receiving, messages of yield and the alert.<br>
Host can never comprehend, as said before, the logic torn.
