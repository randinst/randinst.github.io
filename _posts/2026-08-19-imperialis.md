What in the devil's red hot butthole do these guys want?<br>
One says half of Europe, other—t'be the Middle East's snob bitter aunt<br>
There must be something hidden underneath these piglet orders<br>
Deep and stringent, as to carve t'f out o'these effing borders<br>
Oink, bald piggy said – do this, do that!<br>
Country waits, or w'tever, it's all for this foreign stinking rat!

These wants, what are they if not needs?!<br>
Those needs, they 'quate to fear of personal and private past misdeeds!

Thine pants soiled for years in Soviet dingaling academy?<br>
Doth thy present text o' Wikipedia not compare to prior monster, so you hit me after raising me?

Return to golden cave, Beast Wonderful,<br>
Servants've gathered grapes for you to smear,<br>
Smear atop the other servants, you must smear away the fear<br>
Alone and wishful for a better fiscal year
