Let me try and tell you of worlds dead and live<br>
Wish upon a lightning and see willows from the sky<br>
Gallows, brittle moss, Neptunium and (wh)ores<br>
This sediment we sit on waits for flashing concert lights<br>
And for this observer it leaves nowhere without fight<br>
For the sediment, it spills into the pre-made 'xocrine glands – I wonder why!<br>
Masterworks of song and dance surround the succulent plant demands<br>
Wish upon material possession, up on backs of dangerously feral ants<br>
Yield before court rulings and against the vitriolic fangs<br>
Decisive, but not eager, I watch the match with shirt, pants—meager<br>
Ducks in a pond, raggidy, raggity rocks and sneezes<br>
Eons ago they would have not made freezers<br>
Turnips and silos in an anecdote don't bode together<br>
Sports fan wishes he were lighter than a feather<br>
Alcohol of vinegar and cinnamon of waste tar<br>
Last before not least, your wishes have no way to spar<br>
Harbor idiot there tying knot and naming dots<br>
Cartridges and crevices, they nameless, because engine runs on steams<br>
Otters, when locked up, more cunning than they seem!
