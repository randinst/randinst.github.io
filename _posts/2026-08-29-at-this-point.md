Neutral nonexternal force, it<br>

Eyeing one but horned mistake and donkey,<br>
Form they measly statues with no shape

Not that much unlike spilled yoghurt, rotting but transforming,<br>
Tinsels, their connections, happen interward,<br>
No sobbing...
